Amtavla: Sub Vocal Recognition Project
Luka Rekhviashvili, Giorgi Rurua

Executive Brief

Amtavla develops a wearable Sub Vocal Recognition interface that decodes silent speech from EMG signals and converts biological intention into direct digital action. The central grant objective is to complete Phase 2: build an 8-channel wearable prototype around the ADS1299 capture engine, collect a high-quality multi-user dataset, and validate decoding performance under real-world conditions.

This funding specifically enables fabrication of the custom PCB, acquisition of medical-grade components, participant testing logistics, and accelerated model iteration. Deliverables include a functional multi-channel prototype, a documented data pipeline, benchmarked decoding metrics, and a publishable technical report.
